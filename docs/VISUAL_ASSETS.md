# Mukuru TrustShield visual assets

The cultural background photography is decorative only, displayed at low opacity and never used as evidence or identity data.

All three photographs are sourced from Pexels and marked free to use under the Pexels License at the time they were selected.

1. Johannesburg skyline — Sherissa R  
   Source: https://www.pexels.com/photo/view-of-johannesburg-downtown-south-africa-14660445/

2. African family portrait — Darkshade Photos  
   Source: https://www.pexels.com/photo/african-family-in-traditional-clothing-portrait-33693372/

3. Maputo city skyline — SINAL Multimédia  
   Source: https://www.pexels.com/photo/aerial-view-of-maputo-city-skyline-30188142/

The files are stored locally under public/images/culture/ so the application does not hotlink third-party photography at runtime.

Motion is disabled when prefers-reduced-motion is enabled.
